Welcome

The Psychology of Maturation Framework (PMF) is an independent theoretical research project investigating psychological maturation as a dynamic, non-linear process.

PMF introduces several original theoretical concepts including the 3-in-3 Matrix, State of Continuity, Progressive State, Plasmic State, Plasmic Limit, and Reintegration.

This website serves as the official home of the PMF project, providing publications, research updates, future developments, and project documentation.
