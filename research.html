Research Focus

The Psychology of Maturation Framework (PMF) investigates psychological maturation as a dynamic and non-linear process.

The framework explores relationships among cognition, emotion, experience, psychological development, and adaptive processes.

Current Research

The current stage of the project focuses on developing and refining the theoretical structure of PMF.

  • Development of the 3-in-3 Matrix
  • Development of the State of Continuity
  • Development of the Progressive State
  • Development of the Plasmic State
  • Development of the Plasmic Limit
  • Development of Reintegration

Future Research

Future work will focus on making the framework more testable and examining whether its theoretical concepts can be operationalized and investigated empirically.

  • Development of testable hypotheses
  • Operationalization of PMF concepts
  • Identification of possible research methods
  • Comparison with relevant psychological research
  • Future revisions and extensions of the framework

Research Status

PMF is currently an independent theoretical research project. Its concepts remain theoretical and require empirical investigation before claims about their validity can be established.

Future versions of the framework may incorporate new hypotheses, evidence, and revisions resulting from further research.
