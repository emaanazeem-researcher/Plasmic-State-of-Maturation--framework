What is PMF?

The Psychology of Maturation Framework (PMF) is an independent theoretical framework proposing that psychological maturation is a dynamic, non-linear process rather than a simple consequence of chronological age.

Purpose

PMF seeks to explain how psychological maturation develops through interactions between cognition, emotion, experience, and adaptive processes.

Main Concepts

  • 3-in-3 Matrix
  • State of Continuity
  • Progressive State
  • Plasmic State
  • Plasmic Limit
  • Reintegration
